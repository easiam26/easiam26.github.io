Jeju Island

Jeju Island is one of Korea’s most beautiful and popular destinations, known for its volcanic landscapes, coastal scenery, waterfalls, forests, and unique local culture. The island offers an excellent environment for academic exchange, networking, and relaxation.


Jeju is also recognized for its natural heritage, including Hallasan Mountain, coastal walking trails, lava tubes, and scenic seaside areas. Participants may enjoy the island’s distinctive landscape and culture before or after the conference.


The conference venue is located in Jeju City, which is conveniently accessible from Jeju International Airport. More detailed travel and local information will be updated as the conference approaches.

Local Transportation

Jeju International Airport is the main gateway to the island. Taxi service is usually the most convenient way to reach the conference venue from the airport.
