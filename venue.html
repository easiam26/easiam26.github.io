Venue

Oriental Hotel, Jeju Island, Korea

Visit Hotel Website

The 19th EASIAM Annual Meeting (EASIAM 2026) will be held at the Oriental Hotel on Jeju Island, Korea.

The Oriental Hotel is located near the coast of Jeju City and is conveniently accessible from Jeju International Airport. The hotel will serve as the main conference venue for plenary talks, invited talks, minisymposia, contributed talks, poster sessions, and the banquet.

More detailed information about the venue and transportation will be updated as the conference approaches.

Airport Access

From Jeju International Airport, participants can reach the Oriental Hotel by taxi or local transportation. The hotel is located relatively close to the airport, and taxi travel is expected to be the most convenient option for most participants.
