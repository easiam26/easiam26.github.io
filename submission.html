Abstract Submission

Abstract submission for EASIAM 2026 is now open through Microsoft CMT.

Submission tracks:

1. Minisymposium

This track is only for speakers who have already been invited to speak in an accepted minisymposium. Minisymposium speakers should select the appropriate minisymposium from the list in the CMT system and submit their talk title and abstract.

2. Contributed Talk / Poster

This track is for participants who wish to submit an independent contributed oral presentation or poster presentation.

All presenters are required to enter the title and abstract directly in the CMT submission form. Uploading a LaTeX-generated PDF abstract is optional but recommended if the abstract contains mathematical notation.

Abstract Submission Deadlines:

  • Contributed Talk / Poster: June 14, 2026 (KST)
  • Minisymposium: June 30, 2026 July 15, 2026 (KST)
