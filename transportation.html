Airport Pickup Guide

How to find Shuttle 2 at Jeju International Airport

Whether you arrive on a domestic or international flight, proceed to Gate 2 on Level 1 (Arrivals). You may meet the EASIAM shuttle guide inside Gate 2 or follow the self-guided walking route to the bus.

Step 1

Go to Gate 2 on Level 1

  • Domestic arrivals: Proceed directly to Gate 2 in the main arrivals hall and exit through Gate 2.
  • International arrivals: Follow the indoor corridor toward the main terminal, continue to Gate 2, and exit through Gate 2.
Recommended

Meet the EASIAM shuttle guide

An EASIAM guide will wait inside Gate 2 on Level 1 holding an EASIAM sign. Please arrive at Gate 2 no later than 10 minutes before the scheduled departure time. The guide will walk with participants to the shuttle boarding area.

If you do not see the guide, follow the self-guided route below.

Self-Guided Route

Walk from Gate 2 to the bus parking area

  1. 1
    Exit the terminal through Gate 2 on Level 1.
  2. 2
    Cross the pedestrian crossing toward the parking building directly in front of the terminal.
  3. 3
    Continue past the parking building and walk around to the area behind it.
  4. 4
    Look for the EASIAM shuttle in the bus parking area.

Bus Identification

Confirm that you have found the correct bus
