Travel Awards

About the Travel Awards

EASIAM 2026 will provide a limited number of travel awards to support graduate students and early-career researchers who wish to participate in the East Asia Section of SIAM Annual Meeting 2026 so they can gain the experience and exposure that comes from attending and presenting at SIAM conferences.

Award Support

Selected awardees will receive:

(1) Registration fee waiver
(2) Accommodation support during the conference period, arranged by the EASIAM 2026 Organizing Committee

The number of awards will depend on the available budget and the number of qualified applicants.

Eligibility

Applications are open to:

- Graduate students
- Early-career researchers within 6 years of PhD completion at the time of the conference

Required Documents

Applicants should submit the following materials.

For Student Applicants

(1) Applicant Statement: Short summary of why you are requesting conference support, the rationale for your request (financial need, scholarship, research, etc.), and your interest in the conference. If you are presenting, please include your paper/poster/symposium title and the names of any co-authors/organizers.
(2) CV: An updated copy of your curriculum vitae.
(3) Letter of Recommendation: A letter from your advisor or senior colleague noting the applicant's achievements, potential, and any special circumstances that may justify receiving an award. The recommendation letter should be sent directly by the advisor or letter writer to easiam2026@gmail.com.

For Early-Career Researcher Applicants

(1) Applicant Statement: Short summary of why you are requesting conference support, the rationale for your request (financial need, scholarship, research, etc.), and your interest in the conference. If you are presenting, please include your paper/poster/symposium title and the names of any co-authors/organizers.
(2) CV: An updated copy of your curriculum vitae.
* Letters of recommendation are recommended but NOT required for early career applicants.

Application Deadline

Travel Award Application Deadline: July 17, 2026

How to Apply

Applications should be submitted by email to the Organizing Committee. Please review the submission instructions below:

Email Address: easiam2026@gmail.com

Email Subject Line: EASIAM 2026 Travel Award Application – [Your Full Name]

Attachment: Please send the application statement and CV as a single PDF file.

Recommended File Name: EASIAM2026_TravelAward_[LastName]_[FirstName].pdf

Note for Student Applicants: The advisor’s recommendation letter must be sent separately by the advisor or letter writer to the same email address with the subject line: EASIAM 2026 Travel Award Recommendation – [Applicant Full Name]

Important Notes

  • Applying for a travel award is separate from abstract submission and conference registration.
  • Travel award support is limited and not all qualified applicants can be supported.
  • For any questions, please contact: easiam2026@gmail.com
